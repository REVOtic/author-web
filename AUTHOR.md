# AUTHOR.md - Jyotindra Nath Prasad

## Author Overview
**Full Name:** Jyotindra Nath Prasad  
**Born:** May 9, 1955, in village Enkhaun, Dulhin Bazar, Bihta, Patna district, Bihar, India  
**Profession:** Author, Veteran Journalist (47+ years, including Delhi, Bihar, Chhattisgarh; freelance in Patna)  
**Genres:** Autobiography (3 volumes), Diaries (multiple), Poetry, Spiritual Essays, Satire, Short Stories, Journalism Reflections  
**Languages:** Primarily Hindi and English (21+ books: ~10 English, ~11 Hindi)  
**Key Themes:** Rural-to-urban journey, inner spiritual exploration, fearless truth-seeking in journalism, human resilience, societal critique, hope amid challenges  
**Highlights:** Self-made career without patronage; focus on investigative reporting and soul-searching; eight diary-format books, three poetry collections, three autobiography volumes. Some works available as audiobooks.  
**Website:** [authorjyotindra.com](https://authorjyotindra.com)  
**Amazon Author Page:** [Jyotindra Nath Prasad on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/stores/author/B07T58GRJW)  

Jyotindra Nath Prasad is a profound voice from Bihar, blending raw journalistic honesty with deep spiritual introspection. His writings illuminate personal growth, societal truths, and the eternal quest for meaning, inspiring readers to embrace humanity, resilience, and inner peace.
